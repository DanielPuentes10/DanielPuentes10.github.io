About

whoami

I'm an Application Support Analyst, focused on tracing incidents to their root cause: application, database, and infrastructure layers, not just the symptom. I work daily with C#, .NET, SQL Server, and AWS, and manage Windows servers with PowerShell.

I'm a quiet person, and that's helped me concentrate on programming: learning new things and not giving up during the hardest moments. I really enjoy the challenges programming throws at me to become a better professional.

When I'm not resolving tickets, I'm tinkering with software-defined radios, probing how networks break so I understand how to protect them, or playing guitar. All of it ends up recorded for @DaniTechDev and @trabajosdeprogramacion.
